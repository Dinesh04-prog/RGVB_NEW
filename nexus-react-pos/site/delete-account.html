Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

  • Shop owner: open the menu ☰ → Shop & Cashiers → Delete shop, type the shop name and tap Delete everything. This deletes the shop and all its data (items, bills, udhaar records, cashiers, settings) and your account.
  • Cashier: open the menu ☰ → Delete my account (under Sign Out). You leave the shop and your account is deleted. Bills you made stay with the shop, because they are the shop's records.

For safety, Google asks you to have signed in within the last few minutes. If the app says so, sign out, sign in again and repeat.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email [your contact email] from the Google account you use in Kirana POS, with the subject "Delete my account" and the shop name. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in account and, for an owner, everything stored for the shop in our database.
  • Kept: nothing by us. Copies you made yourself (Excel exports, backup files, bills shared on WhatsApp, printed bills) are not touched.
