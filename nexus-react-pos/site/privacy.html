Kirana POS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Kirana POS is a billing and stock app for small shops. This page explains what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We do not show ads and we do not sell any data.

What the app stores

  • Your Google account: name, email address and profile photo, used to sign you in and to show who made a bill.
  • Shop details entered by the shop owner: shop name, address, phone, WhatsApp number, UPI ID, bill settings and logo.
  • Items: names, prices, stock, barcodes and small item pictures.
  • Bills: items, amounts, date, and the customer's name and mobile number when the shop enters them.
  • Udhaar (credit) records: the customer's name, mobile number, and the amounts owed and paid.
  • Bill approvals sent between a cashier and the owner, and the list of people in each shop.

All of this belongs to the shop. Only people the shop owner has added to the shop can see it.

Where it is kept

  • On the phone, so billing works without internet.
  • In Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), so the shop's phones stay in sync and bills can be restored on a new phone. Security rules let each shop's members reach only their own shop's data.

Photos sent for reading ("Fill from photos")

When someone fills in a new item from photos of its pack, the photos are first read on the phone. If the online reader is turned on, the photos are also sent through our server (Cloudflare Workers) to Google Gemini to read the product name, MRP and pack size. Only pack photos are sent, never bills or customer data. Google may process these photos under the Gemini API terms. Our server does not keep the photos.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and take item photos. Asked only when you scan.
  • Microphone: for voice search of items. Asked only when you use it.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to print bills on a paired Bluetooth printer. Asked only when you choose a printer.

The app does not use your location.

Sharing

We do not sell data or share it with advertisers. Data is processed only by the services named above (Google Firebase, Google Gemini, Cloudflare) to run the app. When the shop sends a bill or a reminder on WhatsApp, it goes through the shop's own WhatsApp.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the shop deletes it. You can delete it yourself in the app:

  • Shop owner: ☰ → Shop & Cashiers → Delete shop. This deletes the shop, all its items, bills, udhaar records and cashiers, and the owner's account.
  • Cashier: ☰ → Delete my account. Bills the cashier made stay with the shop.

Or ask us to delete it: see Delete your account and data.

Children

The app is for shop owners and their staff. It is not meant for children under 13.
